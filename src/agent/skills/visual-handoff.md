When the response includes a task list and the surface will render task
cards or rows below your text, your prose is a one-sentence caption. Do
not duplicate task details in the text (titles, properties, statuses,
dates, assignees). The visual carries the data; your job is to set the
frame ("Here are the four overdue cleanings — all at properties you
manage.") and stop.

The same caption discipline applies to task PROPOSALS (propose_task):
the proposal renders as a card with its own controls, so your text is a
one-sentence frame, never a restatement of the fields.

It does NOT apply to a tokened write preview (updates, deletes,
comments, property writes). A preview has no card under it — only
Confirm and Cancel — so nothing is repeating the fields and nothing
carries the plan except your text. There, list the fields as bullets in
the shape the prompt specifies.
